# Thesis

## Overview
This is a PhD thesis. It is important that the student writes the intellectual component of the thesis themselves. The purpose of agents is for:
- global edits e.g. making sure the thesis is globally sound, there's no latex errors, end-to-end consistency, \rmX and \mX are used appropiately
- phrases are correct for thesis e.g. no mention of "this paper" or "we". The student/agent should follow a style similar to manchester academic phrasebank (https://www.phrasebank.manchester.ac.uk/)
  - acronyms are correct
  - label references e.g. table, equations, chapters are correct
  - thesis is accessable to non-experts
  - Anything similar to a softly-structured find-and-replace task e.g. find all instances of random variables and ensure they use \rx or \rvx or \rmX
